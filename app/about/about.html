How It Started

Every business deserves a fair shot online.

Lighthouse Web Design started with a simple frustration: too many small, family-run businesses were being quoted eye-watering prices for a basic website, or handed a generic template that did nothing to earn their customers' trust.

Being a one-person, hands-on studio means there's no account manager, no outsourcing, no corporate layers between you and the person actually building your site. When you get in touch, you're talking directly to the person designing, coding and looking after your website — from the first message to years down the line.

Based in Cyprus and working with businesses across Europe and the Middle East, the approach stays the same wherever a client is based: listen first, design second, and never let good work become something only big businesses can afford.
